Helping Communities in Need

Gift of the Givers Foundation is a South African humanitarian organisation that provides assistance to people and communities affected by disasters, poverty and difficult situations.

The organisation provides support in areas such as disaster relief, food assistance, healthcare, water provision and community support.

Our Purpose

This website provides information about Gift of the Givers, its humanitarian work, projects and ways that people can support the organisation.
